The Team

MEET OUR TRAINERS

Four certified pros, each with their own style and specialties — find the one who fits your goals. Your first session is on us.

Korina

1-on-1 · Kickboxing · Conditioning

Often the first face you see when you walk in, Korina brings bright, energetic coaching and a variety of training styles to keep things exciting. AFAA certified in Group Fitness & Personal Training, MMA, and Zumba.

Laurie

Strength · Yoga · Weight Loss

Specializes in strength & conditioning, yoga, and mindful, healthy-eating weight loss — empowering clients with the knowledge and support to build a healthier life. NSCA-certified, 200hr Yoga Alliance, AFAA, CPR/First Aid.

Christine

Strength · Mindset · Whole Health

A NASM-certified trainer, published author, and former educator who measures progress by strength and confidence — never a number on the scale. Her mission: helping people feel strong, connected, and capable of more.

Strength · Bodybuilding · All Levels

Training full-time since 2015, Josh works with everyone from teenagers to seniors. A competitive bodybuilder whose passion shows in every session — you'll leave stronger, fitter, and more motivated. W.I.T.S certified, CPR/AED.
